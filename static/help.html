Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about autonomous claim verification with Truth Mirror

How to Get the Best Results

Truth Mirror investigates specific statements against global media wires. Provide clear, bounded claims with dates, locations, or key figures for optimal cross-source synthesis.

01

How Do I use it?

Submit any claim that you heard or saw and the engine will start to verify it. Including specific dates, time periods and areas would help, and focused claims are better than just vague opinions.

02

How long does it take to process a claim?

Analysis typically takes 5-7 minutes depending on claim complexity and server load, as it requires gathering and cross-examining multiple sources globally. Cached claims process instantly.

03

Is there any censorship on what the engine can say or cannot?

No. The engine operates autonomously without censorship rules or political steering. It strictly reflects what the underlying fetched sources state, showing clearly what is supported by evidence, what is unverified, and what is contradicted.

04

Is this tool funded or supported by any organization/nation?

No. This is an independent project not supported or funded by any organization, government, or nation, and is not used to serve any political or national interest.

05

Can the tool be biased towards a specific perspective?

An AI model can inherently inherit bias if it only receives sources from a single viewpoint. We have made a deliberate, engineered effort to eliminate bias by fetching from diverse, balanced wire services across neutral, Western, Eastern, and independent global sources. The engine systematically separates hard verified facts from editorial opinions, allowing you to see the multi-perspective reality rather than a single biased conclusion.
