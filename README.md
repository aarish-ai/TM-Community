# Truth Mirror

> Autonomous Global Claim Verification & Narrative Intelligence

Truth Mirror cross-examines geopolitical statements across diverse global news feeds, state media outlets, and international wire services to expose bias, separate facts from spin, and reconstruct the complete story.

---

## The Problem

In modern geopolitical discourse, sorting fact from narrative framing is increasingly difficult. Major events are covered with vastly different emphases depending on where news originates—some outlets highlight official state statements, others omit critical economic context, and some circulate unverified claims as settled fact.

Traditional fact-checkers often reduce complex geopolitical events to a single binary "True" or "False" label. In reality, statements frequently contain a mixture of established facts, disputed interpretations, and omitted background. Without seeing the underlying sources and regional perspectives, readers cannot judge the full picture for themselves.

## The Solution

Truth Mirror provides an automated, multi-angle intelligence breakdown for any geopolitical claim. Instead of presenting a dogmatic judgment, Truth Mirror:

1. **Retrieves Real-Time Coverage:** Gathers reporting and wire dispatches across Western, non-Western, and international news sources.
2. **Dissects the Statement:** Deconstructs claims into verifiable components and assesses whether each point is confirmed, contradicted, or disputed.
3. **Surfaces Hidden Stories & Omissions:** Uncovers narrative divergences and details that specific media blocs leave out.
4. **Delivers Full Transparency:** Displays every analyzed article, categorizing each source's stance, confidence, and primary emphasis so you can inspect the evidence directly.

---

## Key Features

### 1. Multi-Perspective Claim Verification
Submit any geopolitical statement—from breaking international tensions to policy assertions. Truth Mirror cross-references the statement across dozens of active news feeds and archives to synthesize a clear verdict and overall confidence assessment.

### 2. Three-Tier Fact & Dispute Breakdown
Every analysis separates the findings into actionable clarity:
- **Verified Facts:** Elements confirmed by direct reporting and corroborating evidence across independent sources.
- **Disproven Elements:** Specific points contradicted or refuted by primary data or consensus reporting.
- **Unclear & Disputed Nuances:** Active disagreements, contested figures, or areas where credible sources directly conflict.

### 3. Hidden Stories & Omissions
News outlets frequently shape perception not by what they report, but by what they omit. Truth Mirror identifies diverging storylines, bringing forward context or regional developments that mainstream coverage may have downplayed or overlooked.

### 4. Direct Source Transparency
We do not ask you to take our word for it. Every verification includes a comprehensive, interactive source table showing:
- Direct links to the primary articles.
- The specific stance taken by each publisher (*Confirms*, *Denies*, or *Inconclusive/Background*).
- An extracted summary of each source's key emphasis.

### 5. Quick Runs & Cached Intelligence
Explore recent and common geopolitical investigations with zero latency. Pre-analyzed claims in the instant cache allow immediate review of full verdicts, source tables, and narrative breakdowns.

### 6. Freemium Access
- **Free Plan:** 5 free custom claim analyses per day, unlimited access to cached claims, and complete access to all breakdown panels and source lists.
- **Community Transparency:** The engine's source code is openly published under the AGPL-3.0 license to ensure independent auditability and algorithmic neutrality.

## Verification Integrity & AI Disclaimer

Truth Mirror uses artificial intelligence to ingest, evaluate, and categorize live information from external news feeds. While designed with rigorous retrieval checks:
- **AI systems can make mistakes.** Results reflect current reporting and probabilistic synthesis, not infallible ground truth.
- **Primary sources are always linked.** We strongly encourage users to click through to individual publisher articles to verify context and evidence independently.
- **Community feedback is active.** Users can flag inaccuracies or suggest corrections directly through the integrated feedback channel.
